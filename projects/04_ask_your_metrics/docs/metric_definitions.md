# Metric definitions

## Ending MRR

Sum of monthly-normalized MRR for subscriptions marked active or past due in the selected month. Inactive subscriptions contribute zero. Taxes, free plans and usage charges are outside this schema.

## Active customers

Count of customers with MRR greater than zero in the selected month.

## Customer churn rate

Customers whose prior MRR was positive and current MRR is zero, divided by customers whose prior MRR was positive.

## Net revenue retention

Current MRR from customers with positive prior MRR divided by their prior MRR. It includes expansion, contraction and churn, and excludes new customers from the numerator and denominator.

## Segment MRR

Ending MRR grouped by the authored customer segment.

## Invoice collection rate

Paid invoice amount divided by total invoice amount in the selected month. It is a simple monthly status view, not cash reconciliation or ageing.

## Monthly MRR series

Ending MRR repeated for each month in the requested year.
