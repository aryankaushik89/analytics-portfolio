# Findings

The base data contains 360 fictional merchants, CAD 18.77 million in monthly processed sales and 315,622 transactions.

The Current plan produces CAD 143,763 in contribution margin at a 2.054% weighted effective rate. The Growth plan lowers the weighted effective rate to 1.913% and produces CAD 117,290 in contribution margin. The Simple plan sits between them at a 1.988% effective rate and CAD 131,403 in contribution margin.

The pricing mix matters by segment. Under the Growth plan, the monthly fee makes the Micro segment's effective rate 2.33%, while Enterprise is 1.81%. A lower volume markup does not guarantee a lower effective rate for every merchant.

The cost-shock control shows the main pricing risk. Holding Growth pricing fixed while credit costs rise by 100 basis points changes total contribution margin to negative CAD 13,632 and creates 143 negative-margin merchants. This is a stress test, not a prediction.

The decision is not to apply one plan to every merchant. Current produces the strongest base contribution, Growth can be more competitive for larger merchants, and the segment result shows why contract structure and cost monitoring need to be reviewed together.
