# Project roadmap

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 11 | Finance Reconciliation Desk | Transaction matching, exceptions and financial controls | Planned |
| 12 | Support Operations and Text Insights | Service levels, topic classification and capacity decisions | Planned |
